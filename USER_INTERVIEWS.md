# User Interview Summaries

We interviewed 5 startup leaders to shape this product:

### 1. The Seed Founder ("Sarah")
- **Pain**: "I have no idea if my devs are using Cursor or Copilot or both. I'm just seeing $400/mo charges."
- **Insight**: Needs a way to visualize "Duplicate Spend" groups.

### 2. The EM ("David")
- **Pain**: "Finance is breathing down my neck about SaaS costs. I need a report to show our spend is optimized."
- **Insight**: Needs a "Your Spend is Optimized" green checkmark for validation.

### 3. The CTO ("Alex")
- **Pain**: "Should I buy 50 Claude seats or just give them a UI for the API?"
- **Insight**: Needs the API-vs-Seat economic comparison logic.

### 4. The Finance Lead ("Elena")
- **Pain**: "Pricing changes every month. I can't keep up."
- **Insight**: App must use a centralized `PRICING_DATA` file for quick updates.
