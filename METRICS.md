# Success Metrics (KPIs)

## Product Health
1. **Audit Completion Rate**: % of users who start the form and reach the results page. Target: >70%.
2. **Mean Savings Identified**: Average $ monthly savings per audit. Target: >$200/mo.
3. **Audit Complexity**: Average number of tools entered per audit. Target: 2.5.

## Growth & Virality
1. **Share Rate**: % of users who copy the share link or screenshot results. Target: >15%.
2. **Viral Coefficient (K-factor)**: New users per existing user. Target: >0.2.

## Business Impact
1. **Lead Conversion Rate**: % of users who provide their email after the audit. Target: >10%.
2. **Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs)**: Leads with >$500/mo savings identified.
